Interaction notes
Selection
Edit tool: hovering a line shows a 1px dashed outline; tap or click opens the inline editor in place with the caret at the tapped spot. The line gets a navy 2px ring and a 5px soft halo; the rest of the page stays untouched.
Added objects: a single tap selects (blue outline, corner dots, resize knob bottom-right). Tap empty paper or press Esc to deselect. Shift-click adds to the selection on desktop.
Delete / Backspace removes the selection; Ctrl+D duplicates with an 8px offset.
Dragging and resizing
Drag the object body or the move handle in its toolbar. On touch, dragging starts after 4px of travel so taps never nudge things.
While dragging, the floating toolbar fades out and alignment guides (1px, Selection blue) snap to page centre and margins within 6px. Arrow keys move 1pt, Shift+arrow 10pt.
The resize knob keeps aspect ratio for signatures and images; Alt/Option frees it. Text boxes resize the box and reflow; font size changes only through the stepper.
Zoom
Pinch zooms around the midpoint between fingers; two-finger pan scrolls. Double-tap toggles fit-width and 200% at the tapped point. One-finger drag on empty desk scrolls, never draws (except with Draw active).
Desktop: Ctrl/⌘+wheel zooms at the cursor, Ctrl+0 fits width, the zoom pill shows tabular %. Range 25–400%, steps 25/50/75/100/125/150/200/300/400.
Objects and handles keep constant screen size at every zoom level.
Where panels appear
Mobile 390: tools in a scrollable bottom dock. Editing text swaps the dock for a slim properties bar above the keyboard; the page auto-scrolls so the edited line sits in the top third. Caret ^ expands the bar into a half sheet. Dialogs are bottom sheets.
Tablet 820: left rail (reachable with the left thumb in both orientations). Properties float as a card bottom-right, moving to bottom-left if it would overlap the selection.
Desktop 1440: rail left, 320px properties panel right showing the current selection or the change list.
Large 1920+: adds a page-thumbnail column; the page grows to 780px max instead of stretching.
Motion
Tool switch: marker fill crossfades 180ms ease-out. Panels and sheets rise 8px + fade in 220–260ms; close is 160ms. Toasts enter from below, stay 4s. All motion drops to opacity-only under prefers-reduced-motion.
