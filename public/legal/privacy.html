2. Ihre Dokumente und Fotos

Alles, was Sie mit Paginka öffnen, bearbeiten, scannen, unterschreiben oder umwandeln (PDFs, Bilder, Office- und Textdateien, Kamerafotos, Unterschriften), wird direkt in Ihrem Browser bzw. in der App auf Ihrem Gerät verarbeitet. 3. Speicherung auf Ihrem Gerät

Damit die App wie gewünscht funktioniert, speichert sie folgende Daten nur lokal in Ihrem Browser bzw. in der App (localStorage, IndexedDB, Offline-Cache). Diese Daten verlassen Ihr Gerät nicht:

  • Einstellungen: helles/dunkles Design, Seitenleiste, zuletzt benutzte Werkzeuge und Farben, Scan-Einstellungen;
  • „Zuletzt verwendet“: Name, Seitenzahl, Vorschaubild und eine Kopie zuletzt geöffneter oder bearbeiteter Dokumente, damit Sie sie wieder öffnen können;
  • Unterschriften, wenn Sie „Auf diesem Gerät speichern“ wählen;
  • die Programmdateien der App (Offline-Cache), damit sie auch ohne Internet funktioniert.

Diese Speicherung ist für die von Ihnen ausdrücklich gewünschten Funktionen technisch erforderlich (§ 165 Abs. 3 TKG 2021; Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b und f DSGVO). Es werden keine Cookies gesetzt. Sie können die Daten jederzeit löschen: einzelne Dokumente über das „×“ in der Liste „Zuletzt verwendet“, gespeicherte Unterschriften im Unterschriften-Dialog, oder alles über die Browser-Einstellungen („Websitedaten löschen“) bzw. in Android unter Einstellungen › Apps › Paginka › Speicher › „Daten löschen“.

In short: your documents are processed only on your device. They are never uploaded, sent to us or anyone else, or stored by us. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no cookies.

2. Your documents and photos

Everything you open, edit, scan, sign or convert (PDFs, images, Office and text files, camera photos, signatures) is processed directly in your browser or in the app on your device, including text recognition (OCR) and scan clean-up. We never receive this content. When you save, share or print a result, or hand it to another app, your device does this to the destination you choose.

3. Storage on your device

To work as you ask, the app stores the following only locally (localStorage, IndexedDB, offline cache); it never leaves your device: settings (theme, panels, recent tools and colours, scan options); “Recent” documents (name, page count, thumbnail and a copy, so you can reopen them); signatures, if you choose “Save on this device”; and the app's own files for offline use. This storage is strictly necessary for functions you request (§ 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021; Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). No cookies are set. You can delete it at any time: single documents with the “×” in the Recent list, signatures in the signature dialog, or everything by clearing site data in your browser or, on Android, Settings › Apps › Paginka › Storage › Clear data.

4. Website hosting

The web app is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, a Microsoft company). When you open the website, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular your IP address, date and time, the requested file and browser/system information, to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in secure, reliable delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub relies on the safeguards described in its privacy statement (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or EU standard contractual clauses). We do not receive access logs. See the GitHub privacy statement. Your documents are not transferred: the website only loads the program files; processing then happens on your device.

5. Android app

Permissions: Camera — only for scanning documents when you open the scanner; photos stay on the device. Internet — not needed to load the app's content (it is bundled); the app itself sends none of your data. Files you pass to Paginka with “Open with” or “Share” are read only on the device. For dates, the app opens your calendar app with a pre-filled event; Paginka has no access to your calendar.

Google document scanner (Google Play services): on Android devices with Google Play services, the scanner uses Google's document scanner (ML Kit), provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Detection and page processing happen on the device and the scanned images are not sent to Google. ML Kit does send technical diagnostic data to Google (such as device and app information, device identifiers, performance metrics, error codes, and the type and version of the feature used) to operate and improve the service; it is encrypted in transit and, according to Google, not shared with third parties. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a reliable scan function (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See ML Kit data disclosure and the Google privacy policy. If Google's scanner is not available, the app uses its own scanner, which works without Google.

Getting the app: when you download the app from Google Play or GitHub, those providers process the data needed for this under their own privacy terms.
